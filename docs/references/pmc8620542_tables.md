## 正文关键句（2段相对1段的法定差异，引 EU 立法）

> "The major differences of follow-up formula composition, as compared to infant formula, are an **increased iron content (0.5–2.0 vs. 0.3–1.3 mg/100 kcal)** and a **lower minimum protein content (1.6 vs. 1.8 g/100 kcal)**, matching the changing requirements over time [6,18]."

Codex 对 follow-up formula 的定义："a food intended for use as a liquid part of the weaning diet for the infant from the 6th month on and for young children"（引 CXS 156/GSIF 合并文本）。
